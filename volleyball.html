About Volleyball

Volleyball is a dynamic team sport where two teams of six players are separated by a net. The objective is to send the ball over the net and ground it on the opponent's court while preventing the same effort by the opponent. Invented by William G. Morgan in 1895 in Massachusetts, volleyball has grown into one of the most popular participation sports worldwide, played both indoors and on beaches.

Teams are allowed up to three touches to return the ball across the net. A match is typically played as best of five sets, with the first four sets played to 25 points and a potential fifth set played to 15 points. A team must win by at least two points. Volleyball emphasizes quick reflexes, vertical jumping ability, teamwork, and strategic positioning.

Getting Started

Ready to start playing volleyball? Here are some tips for beginners:

  • Learn the basics: Practice passing, setting, serving, and basic hitting techniques
  • Find a court: Look for indoor gyms, recreation centers, or beach volleyball courts
  • Get the right gear: Volleyball shoes with good ankle support and comfortable athletic wear
  • Watch and learn: Study professional matches to understand rotations and team strategies
  • Join a league: Find recreational leagues or pick-up games to practice team play
