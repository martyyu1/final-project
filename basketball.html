About Basketball

Basketball is a team sport played on a rectangular court where two teams of five players compete to score points by shooting a ball through the opponent's hoop. Invented by Dr. James Naismith in 1891 in Springfield, Massachusetts, basketball has become one of the most popular sports worldwide. The game emphasizes speed, agility, teamwork, and strategic play.

A standard game consists of four quarters, each lasting 12 minutes in professional leagues like the NBA. The team with the most points at the end of regulation wins. Basketball is played at various levels including youth leagues, high school, college, and professional competitions around the globe.

Getting Started

Ready to start playing basketball? Here are some tips for beginners:

  • Learn the basics: Practice dribbling, shooting, and passing fundamentals
  • Find a court: Look for local gyms, parks, or community centers with basketball courts
  • Get the right gear: A good pair of basketball shoes and comfortable athletic wear
  • Watch and learn: Study professional games to understand strategy and technique
  • Join a team: Find local leagues or pick-up games to practice with others
