About Tennis

Tennis is an elegant racket sport that can be played individually against a single opponent (singles) or between two teams of two players each (doubles). Players use a strung racket to strike a hollow rubber ball covered with felt over a net and into the opponent's court. The sport originated in 19th-century England and has evolved into one of the most prestigious individual sports worldwide.

A tennis match is composed of points, games, and sets. Players must win at least four points to win a game, six games to win a set, and typically two or three sets to win a match. Tennis is unique in its scoring system (love, 15, 30, 40) and requires a combination of physical fitness, technical skill, strategic thinking, and mental fortitude to excel.

Getting Started

Ready to start playing tennis? Here are some tips for beginners:

  • Learn the basics: Practice proper grip, footwork, and fundamental strokes (forehand, backhand, serve)
  • Find a court: Look for public tennis courts at parks, schools, or community centers
  • Get the right gear: A quality tennis racket, comfortable tennis shoes, and tennis balls
  • Watch and learn: Study professional matches to understand shot selection and court positioning
  • Take lessons: Consider working with a coach to develop proper technique from the start
